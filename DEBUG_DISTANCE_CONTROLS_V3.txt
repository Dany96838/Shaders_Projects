Debug distance controls:
- Waving Render Distance
- Water Wave Distance
- Water Effects Distance (reflections/refraction/caustics)
- Godray Distance
- Volumetric Lighting Distance
- Bloom Distance

0 = Unlimited. These settings only limit shader-side effects. They do not replace Minecraft render distance or Sodium/Iris occlusion/frustum culling.

Existing distance settings such as Shadows Render Distance and POM Render Distance remain available in their original menus.
